# LinkedIn 客户开发手册
> 更新于 2026-04-13

---

## 一、两类目标买家

### 路径A：礼品经销商（Promotional Products Distributor）
- **画像**：Owner / Director / Sourcing Manager，1–20人小型经销商
- **所在地**：澳大利亚、英国、德国、荷兰
- **特征**：已有企业客户资源，在找差异化产品，关注价格+交期+品控
- **优先级**：现阶段主攻，成交周期4–8周

### 路径B：企业直采买家
- **画像**：HR Manager / Office Manager / Marketing Manager，200人以上公司
- **行业优先**：科技（SaaS）、金融、房地产、咨询
- **特征**：有固定采购节点（年底、公司周年、入职季）
- **优先级**：内容积累5–8篇后跟进，成交周期3–6个月

## 二、如何判断对方有购买需求

### 时间节点信号
- 公司宣布融资、上市、周年庆 → 庆典礼品需求
- Q4（10–12月） → 年终客户礼品季
- 新员工入职季（1–3月） → 入职礼包需求

### LinkedIn 帖子信号
- 发帖晒员工活动、团建、客户答谢 → 已有礼品文化
- 发帖说 "We're hiring" / "Team is growing" → 可能有入职礼包需求
- 发帖晒收到或送出礼品 → 直接证明在做这件事

### 结构性有需求的行业（不用判断，直接加）
| 行业 | 原因 |
|------|------|
| 科技公司（SaaS） | 客户成功团队有礼品预算 |
| 房地产中介 | 成交礼是标配 |
| 会计/律所 | 年终客户答谢 |
| 医疗器械销售 | 医院/诊所拜访礼 |
| 企业培训/咨询 | 学员结业礼包 |

## 四、加人话术

### 企业直采 — SaaS/科技公司

**情况A：对方发过礼品相关帖子**
> Hi [Name], saw your post about [员工入职/客户答谢] — looks like you put real thought into the experience. I source custom metal gift sets for SaaS teams (MOQ 100, packaging included). Not a pitch — just connecting with people who clearly care about this stuff.

**情况B：对方是HR，公司在招人**
> Hi [Name], noticed [公司名] is growing the team — congrats. I work with HR leads at tech companies on custom metal onboarding kits. Happy to connect if it's ever relevant.

---

### 礼品经销商

**情况A：对方有产品目录但缺金属品类**
> Hi [Name], came across [公司名] — solid catalog. Noticed you cover a lot of print and textile, but less on the metal side. I source custom metal gift sets from China — brass, titanium, stainless steel, MOQ 100 sets. If a client brief ever calls for something more premium, happy to quote. Good to connect.

**情况B：对方发过 sourcing 相关帖子**
> Hi [Name], saw you're active in the promo products space. I supply custom metal gift sets to distributors — MOQ 100, full packaging customization, 25–35 day production. If you're ever looking for a reliable metal gift source, worth a conversation. Good to connect.

**情况C：通用冷加**
> Hi [Name], I supply custom metal gift sets to promotional products distributors — brass, titanium, stainless steel, MOQ 100. Based in China, work with buyers in AU and EU. Happy to connect if there's ever a fit.

## 五、接受连接后的跟进

### 等2–3天再发第一条消息（不要立刻推销）

**企业直采**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. What kind of gifting programs does [公司名] typically run — annual, event-based, or both?

**经销商**
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. Quick question — when clients come to you asking for something more premium than standard promo merchandise, what's usually the brief? Trying to understand where metal sets fit in the market from a distributor's perspective.

---

### 对方回应后的跟进

**经销商说"有需求但找不到合适供应商"**
> That's exactly the gap I work in. Most factories want 500+ MOQ — I built Wischos specifically around 100 sets, with full branding and packaging included. If you have a brief coming up, I'm happy to put together a quote. What materials do your clients usually ask for?

**经销商说"目前没有需求"**
> Understood — good to know the landscape. I'll send you the catalog link in case something comes up: wischosgift.com. Feel free to reach out anytime.

## 六、整套操作节奏

```
发现信号帖 → 加人（附一句话）→ 等接受 → 2天后发问题
→ 对方回应 → 自然引导到"我可以帮你做这个"
```

- 每天操作：10–15个加人请求
- 优先顺序：经销商 → 有信号的企业买家 → 无信号的企业买家
- 内容发到5–8篇后再大量加企业买家

---

## 七、何时开始加人

| 阶段 | 动作 |
|------|------|
| 现在（0篇内容） | 先发内容，评论目标买家帖子 |
| 发了3–4篇 | 开始加经销商 |
| 发了5–8篇 | 加企业直采买家 |
| 有人互动了你的内容 | 立刻加他，成功率最高 |
