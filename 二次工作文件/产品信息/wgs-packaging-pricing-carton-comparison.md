## Estimate Notes

- Prices are reference ranges for inquiry and budgeting, not final supplier quotes.
- Gift box prices assume custom Wischos logo, black premium paper or black wrap, rigid board construction, and normal export-gift quality.
- EVA prices assume black flocked EVA, 38-45 kg/m3, die-cut/CNC cut slots, and pickup half-moon holes.
- EVA tooling/mold fee is listed separately and is not amortized into the unit packaging cost.
- Unit packaging cost includes gift box + EVA insert + outer carton share + small packing materials. It excludes the products themselves.
- Export cartons assume 5-layer corrugated cartons unless otherwise noted.
- Public online prices vary widely by MOQ, size, material, and region. Made-in-China listing prices are often low lead-in ranges; Uline is a US stock-packaging benchmark and is generally much higher than China factory custom pricing.

## 6. Recommendation

For export quotation:

1. Use the flexible carton plan when shipping by air, express, or small mixed orders.
2. Use the tiered carton plan if you want a simpler purchasing system but still care about freight efficiency.
3. Use the single unified carton only when the priority is warehouse/carton standardization or sea freight consolidation.

For supplier RFQ, ask every box supplier to quote these same columns:

- Gift box outer size and inner usable size.
- Greyboard thickness and paper wrap specification.
- Logo process: silver foil, deboss/emboss, UV, or printing.
- EVA density, flocking, thickness, slot drawing, and pickup holes.
- EVA tooling/mold fee and whether it is refunded after bulk order.
- Outer carton material: 5-layer, bursting/ECT strength, print mark, and packed gross weight limit.
- MOQ price breaks: 500 / 1,000 / 3,000 sets.
