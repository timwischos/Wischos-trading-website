# Wischos Gift — Productized Metal Gift Sets + Custom Metal Support

**For branded merchandise agencies, promotional distributors, and corporate gift partners.**

Wischos Gift supplies productized metal gift set directions and supports selected custom metal components when a brief needs something beyond standard catalogue items.

We are not a broad promotional products catalogue and we are not trying to replace your daily supplier network. The best fit is a planned premium metal gift brief where the client wants a more durable, higher-perceived-value option than a standard giveaway.

## Where We Fit

| Client brief | How Wischos can help |
|---|---|
| A distributor needs a ready premium metal gift option | Quote from productized WGS-style metal set directions |
| A client likes the set idea but needs adaptation | Adjust component mix, finish, logo method, packaging and budget |
| A premium kit needs one stronger metal item | Support the metal component inside the kit |
| A recognition or sponsor project needs weight and permanence | Provide metal keepsake / badge / medallion directions |
| A campaign needs something beyond catalogue | Translate the idea into material, process, MOQ and cost options |
