## Price Assumptions

- Price is based on the specification above.
- Freight, duties, local taxes and destination charges are excluded unless stated.

- Tooling fee may change after final drawing review.
- Final unit price may change if weight, size, finish, packaging or logo method changes.

## Suggested Client Reply Structure

1. Confirm the product direction.
2. State the main assumptions.
3. Give 2 options if possible: cost-efficient and premium.
4. Explain tooling/sample lead time.
5. Ask for artwork, quantity confirmation, deadline and destination.
