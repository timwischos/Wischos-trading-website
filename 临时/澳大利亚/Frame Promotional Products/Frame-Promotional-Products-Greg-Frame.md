- **产品配对**: WGS-005（Executive Set）+ WGS-008（The Quartet）；FOB $18–26/套
- **Hook sentence**: *"Your government and university clients are probably spending more than they need to on year-end recognition gifts — we produce curated 3–4 piece metal sets at FOB prices that give you room to price competitively and still earn a proper margin."*

### Angle 2（稳健）: 金属笔品类成本升级 — Premium Upgrade

- **产品配对**: WP-101~104 金属笔系列；或组合礼盒方案
- **Hook sentence**: *"If you're currently buying metal pens from a local distributor, we can likely put the same quality — or better — in your hands at a cost that gives you 15–20 more points of margin per order."*
- **风险**: 笔类单品 MOQ 100 件可行，但若 Frame 月均销量小，需讨论季度合并订单方案

### Angle 3（长期）: 新买主备档 — Future Owner Seed
