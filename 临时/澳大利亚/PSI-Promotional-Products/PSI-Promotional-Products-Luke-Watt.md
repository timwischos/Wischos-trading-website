## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: Category Gap — 补premium curated gift set层
- **为何成立**: 目录有金属笔单品，但无multi-piece curated metal gift set。企业客户对入职礼包/年末礼品要求越来越完整，单支笔不够用了；Wischos套件填这个gap
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 Executive Set / WGS-006 Onboarding Kit
- **Hook sentence**: *"You carry metal pens — we do curated sets (pen + stand + carry clip in one box) that Australian distributors use to win executive onboarding and recognition briefs at USD $25–35 FOB."*
- **风险**: 若PSI客户预算偏低（单件$5-15），套件价格可能偏高；首次接触先探预算段

### Angle 2（稳健）: Q3-Q4 Timing — 年末礼品季前置接触
- **为何成立**: 澳洲企业Q4礼品从7-8月开始briefing；5-6月接触=在briefing窗口前占好位置
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 / WGS-006（入门套件，FOB $18-25）
- **Hook sentence**: *"Q4 corporate gifting briefs start moving in July-August in Australia — wanted to get our metal gift sets on your radar before the season."*
- **风险**: PSI现有Q4供应商可能已固定；定位为"新briefs"而非"替换现有"

### Angle 3（长期）: Specialist Backup — 金属礼品专项供应商
- **为何成立**: 小公司供应链单点风险高；一个专注金属礼品的直接供应商（vs. 泛化代理）有差异化价值
- **产品配对**: 全金属品类，WGS-001/002作为入门选项
- **Hook sentence**: *"We're a specialist metal gift supplier — not a generalist — so when a brief comes in for engraved executive sets, we quote in 24 hours and produce in 25–35 days."*
- **风险**: 感觉像"backup pitch"，只在Angle 1-2无回应时使用
