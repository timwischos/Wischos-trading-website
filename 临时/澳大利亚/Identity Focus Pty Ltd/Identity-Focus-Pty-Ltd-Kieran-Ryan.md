### 潜在采购需求

- 酒类/啤酒/葡萄酒品牌促销：metal bottle openers, bottle tags, keyrings, custom tokens, tin cases.
- 金融/保险/企业礼品：metal pen gift sets, desk accessories, card holders, executive gift boxes.
- 体育/慈善/活动项目：medals, pins, commemorative metal pieces, custom event tokens.
- POS / trade loader / on-pack：compact custom-shaped metal items, branded metal tags, keyrings, tin cases.

4. 常规目录供应商未必愿意做 MOQ 100 左右的项目型金属定制开发。
