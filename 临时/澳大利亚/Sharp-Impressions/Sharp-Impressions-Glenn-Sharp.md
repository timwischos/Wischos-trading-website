## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: 填补金属礼品套件品类空白

- **为何成立**: 目录显示金属笔、name badges、plaques，但无策划好的多件金属套件。Perth企业客户（尤其矿业）有明确的高端礼品需求，单品无法满足。
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 Executive Desk Set / WGS-008 The Quartet（4件套）/ WGS-001
- **Hook sentence**: *"I noticed Sharp Impressions covers a strong range of branded apparel and recognition products — I wanted to introduce curated metal gift sets that could sit alongside your existing offerings for clients with executive or year-end gifting briefs."*
- **风险**: 可能当前无积极的套件需求；apparel优先

### Angle 2（稳健）: 金属blank + 现有装饰工艺

- **为何成立**: 已有debossing/hot stamping = 金属礼品标准定制工艺。Wischos供应金属笔/桌面件作为blank，他们装饰后加价卖给客户，可增加现有服务边际利润。
- **产品配对**: WP-101~104金属笔单品 / WP-201~208桌面件 / EDC单件
- **Hook sentence**: *"Given that you already offer debossing and hot stamping, your clients could be a natural fit for metal gift pieces — pens, desk accessories, travel sets — where the decoration is already in your toolkit and the supply is the gap."*
- **风险**: 需要其主动采购和管理blank库存，门槛略高

### Angle 3（长期）: Prodir替代 — 更高利润的金属笔路线

- **为何成立**: 已卖Prodir（AUD $8–15/支），证明客户愿意付高端笔价格。Wischos金属笔FOB可提供同档次品质 + 更高distributor利润空间。
- **产品配对**: WP-101~104金属笔，可打包进笔礼盒
- **Hook sentence**: *"You carry Prodir — which tells me your clients value quality writing instruments. I supply custom metal pens from verified Chinese factories at FOB pricing that gives you stronger margin on the same premium positioning."*
- **风险**: Prodir品牌忠诚度可能强；部分客户指名要Prodir
