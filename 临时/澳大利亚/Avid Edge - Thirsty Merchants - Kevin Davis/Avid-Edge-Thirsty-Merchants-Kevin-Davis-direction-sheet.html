Avid Edge already covers corporate merch packs, event gear, drinkware, uniforms, workwear, print and branded merchandise. Thirsty Merchants adds a strong brewery, bar, hospitality and festival merch angle. The best fit for Wischos is not replacing those supplier relationships, but supporting special metal projects when a client wants something more permanent, material-led or boxed.

Focus: brass, stainless and titanium pieces for corporate packs, golf days, brewery merch and VIP event gifts. Not for PPE, apparel, print, ordinary glassware or rush local decoration.
Six Direction Options

Corporate Premium Metal Kit

For client appreciation, executive packs and higher-value corporate merch sets.

  • Brass pen or desk writing piece
  • Weighted stainless tumbler or titanium drinkware
  • Device stand, card holder or boxed desk set

Onboarding / Staff Welcome Kit

For HR, operations and multi-site teams that need useful first-week items.

  • Metal pen or inkless pen
  • RFID card / badge holder
  • Desk tumbler or compact desk piece

Golf Event Keepsakes

For golf days, sponsor gifts and event merchandise above standard apparel.

  • Metal bag tag or divot tool direction
  • Brass pen for sponsor packs
  • Stainless tumbler or bottle opener

Brewery / Bar Metal Merch

For Thirsty Merchants taproom, brewery, festival and hospitality clients.

  • Custom metal opener or bar blade
  • Metal coaster or growler tag
  • Key tag, loyalty tag or VIP pack piece

Field / Site Appreciation Kit

For construction, infrastructure and field teams as gift items, not safety compliance gear.

  • Tactical-style metal pen
  • Compact EDC keychain or key organizer
  • Drinkware plus simple boxed presentation

Grouped Metal Production Run

For co-op production or shared specs across similar client briefs.

  • One product family and one logo area
  • Consistent finish and packaging format
  • Shared MOQ planning across grouped demand
How It Fits Your Existing Model
You keep the client relationship. Wischos supports the product build, material direction, production and export side.
Your local strengths stay local. Print, apparel, fulfilment, activation and account service remain with your team.
The metal gap gets covered. Best used for premium metal pieces, boxed kits and outside-catalogue briefs.
