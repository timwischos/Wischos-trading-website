## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: Premium Metal Pen Tier-Up
- **为何成立**: CNC 已有金属笔品类，说明客户有需求；30年经验意味着他知道普通金属笔被留下的概率 vs 高档黄铜/钛笔的差异
- **产品配对**: WP-101~104（黄铜/不锈钢笔）；可带客户 logo 激光雕刻
- **Hook sentence**: *"You already carry metal pens — I can offer a tier up in brass and titanium for the clients who want something that actually gets kept on a desk."*
- **风险**: 他现有供应商可能已有这个档次；需探明当前采购价格区间

### Angle 2（稳健）: Stainless Drinkware — Lasting Promo
- **为何成立**: 官网有 drinkware 品类；澳大利亚 2025-2026 趋势：stainless bottles 需求 +15%；金属持久性 = 品牌长期曝光
- **产品配对**: WP-401~406（不锈钢饮品器皿）；可刻字 logo
- **Hook sentence**: *"Stainless drinkware keeps the brand in someone's hand every day — the kind of item that sticks around long after the event."*
- **风险**: 竞争激烈（stainless bottles 是澳洲 promo 高度商品化品类）；需主打材质/工艺差异

### Angle 3（长期）: Executive Gift Sets for Corporate Clients
- **为何成立**: CNC 有 executive gifts 品类；其企业客户有年终礼品/VIP 礼品需求；套件单价高 → 利润好
- **产品配对**: WGS-005（Executive Set）/ WGS-008（Quartet）
- **Hook sentence**: *"For clients who want a complete executive gift — pen, desk piece and drinkware in one box — I can source or build a set around their brief."*
- **风险**: 套件 MOQ 100 sets；需确认他是否有这类大批量客户
