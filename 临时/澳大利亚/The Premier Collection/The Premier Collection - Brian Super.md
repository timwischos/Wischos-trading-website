**优先方向：**
- Executive metal gift sets：黄铜笔 + 名片盒 / 书签 / 桌面件 + 礼盒。
- Custom metal keyrings / bottle openers：适合 real estate、automotive、brewery/event sponsor、membership gifts。
- Titanium / stainless EDC gifts：工具笔、钛 EDC、小型金属工具，适合 mining、construction、outdoor、tech hardware。
- Premium desk / recognition pieces：不锈钢桌杯、金属镇纸、定制金属纪念件，适合 recognition、anniversary、VIP events。

**不建议首轮主推：**
- 普通 insulated bottle / tumbler。

- 全目录式供应商介绍。

## 三个切入角度

### Angle 1: Premium custom metal category for distributors

- **为什么有效**: Premier 服务分销商，已有普通 promo stock；Wischos 可补充更有材质感、适合 VIP / executive / recognition 的目录外金属礼品。
- **Hook sentence**: *"I noticed Premier has been active with APPA shows and new product launches, so the fit I see is a premium custom metal category for distributor-led VIP and recognition projects."*
- **风险**: 不要像普通供应商推全目录；只谈 category direction。

### Angle 2: Custom keyrings / bottle openers / small metal pieces

- **为什么有效**: Premier 已有 keyrings 和 custom 类目，金属定制小件与其目录自然相邻。
- **Hook sentence**: *"For briefs that need something outside standard catalogue keyrings or openers, we can build custom metal pieces around the artwork, finish and packaging."*
- **风险**: 需先确认 MOQ、图档复杂度和交期，不主动承诺低价急单。

### Angle 3: Executive gift sets outside the stocked range

- **为什么有效**: 普通单品难差异化，套件更适合高客单 executive / real estate / finance / legal gifting。
- **Hook sentence**: *"Instead of replacing stock items, we can help build small executive metal gift sets when distributors need a more premium option for corporate clients."*
- **风险**: 需要 1-page direction sheet 展示，而不是第一轮报价。
