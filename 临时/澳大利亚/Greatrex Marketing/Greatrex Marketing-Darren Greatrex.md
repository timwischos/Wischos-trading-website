## 公司概况

Darren Greatrex is an experienced sales & marketing strategist in the promotional merchandise, print and event management industry. He joined the industry in Adelaide during 1990.
In 1996 Darren managed the Lion Nathan Breweries merchandise program (Tooheys, Swan, XXXX) and the Sydney 2000 Games merchandise program. 
In 2000 started Profile Sports Marketing (AFL licenses).
Darren has set up his own independent company Greatrex Marketing specialising in corporate apparel, promotional products, point of sale material. Greatrex Marketing have their own in-house heat press machines.
Darren has over 20 years’ experience and is actively involved with several charities including Variety the Children’s Charity (current Chair of SA Variety Bash).
Greatrex Marketing Services offer Global Sourcing, Event Management, branding solutions.
