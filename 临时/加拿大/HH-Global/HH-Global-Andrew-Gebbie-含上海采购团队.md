## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: Q4时间窗 — 立即有效

- **为何成立**: HH Global客户Q4礼品briefing通常6–8月发起。现在（5月底）接触，比竞争供应商早入场。时间紧迫性真实、不矫情。
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 Executive Set + WGS-008 The Quartet（高管/员工认可）
- **Hook sentence**: *"With Q4 executive gifting programs typically briefed in June–August, I wanted to put a direct-factory curated metal gift set option in front of you before client briefs are locked."*
- **风险**: 对方可能已有Q4供应商；应对：定位为填补metal specialist空白，非替换

### Angle 2（稳健）: 直供专项 vs. 聚合商

- **为何成立**: HH Global当前顶级中国供应商是Shanghai Allroad（泛型聚合商）。Wischos直供阳江/东莞金属专项工厂，在刻字深度/表面处理/定制spec控制上有实质优势。
- **产品配对**: 任何金属礼品套件；强调"custom metal specialist"定位
- **Hook sentence**: *"Most branded merchandise programs source metal gifts through generalist aggregators — we work direct with specialist factories in Yangjiang and Dongguan, which means tighter spec control and typically 15–20% better FOB pricing on custom metal sets."*
- **风险**: 需要有实质价格/质量证明；不能只是声明

### Angle 3（长期）: ESG-ready供应商定位

- **为何成立**: HH Global自身EcoVadis Gold + Sustainable Procurement Framework，要求供应商ESG对齐。主动提供工厂ESG文件的供应商减少其合规摩擦，有差异化吸引力。
- **产品配对**: 全产品线；定位为"responsible sourcing partner"
- **Hook sentence**: *"Given HH Global's Sustainable Procurement Framework, I've prepared factory environmental and labor documentation as part of our supplier introduction — most suppliers make you ask for this."*
- **风险**: Wischos目前ESG文件可能薄弱；需提前准备工厂声明再使用此角度
