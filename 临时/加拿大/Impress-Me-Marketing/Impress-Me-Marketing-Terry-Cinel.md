## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: Premium Metal Gift Set — 目录品类补充

- **为何成立**: 他现有SAGE目录覆盖的笔/水杯多为中低档塑料混材；全金属套件（WGS-005/006）是他可向企业客户upsell的差异化品类
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 Executive Set（FOB ~$22-28/set）、WGS-006 Onboarding Set，MOQ 100 sets
- **Hook sentence**: *"Most Canadian distributors I speak to carry pens and drinkware separately — a few have started offering curated metal gift sets to corporate clients as a step up, and it's opened a different tier of buyer."*
- **风险**: 如果他的客户主要是非营利/活动，价格点可能不符；先确认是否有企业客户

### Angle 2（稳健）: 金属笔线延伸

- **为何成立**: Writing instruments是其官网命名分类；Wischos全金属笔（黄铜/钛合金）是他现有笔类目的高端延伸，可打"企业高管礼品级"定位
- **产品配对**: WP-101 Titanium Rollerball / WP-104 Brass Fountain Pen，MOQ 100 pcs，FOB $8-14/pc
- **Hook sentence**: *"I noticed writing instruments are a core part of your range — we specialize in full-metal pens (brass barrel, titanium clip, laser-engraved) that a handful of Canadian distributors have been adding as a 'gift-tier' option for corporate accounts."*
- **风险**: 他可能已有满意的笔类供应商；不要在建立信任前过度推spec

### Angle 3（长期）: Q4季节性企业礼品套件

- **为何成立**: Q4是加拿大promo分销商最高峰；预组装金属套件=一个SKU、一个Logo文件、直接到客户手上，减少Terry的整合工作
- **产品配对**: WGS-008 The Quartet（4件套金属），建议Terry对2-3个企业客户预售后再下单
- **Hook sentence**: *"Q4 is when corporate clients want something more than a branded pen — a curated metal set ships as one SKU, requires one logo file, and lands at the client's office gift-ready."*
- **风险**: 5月提Q4略早；作为规划预热，不作冷开场
