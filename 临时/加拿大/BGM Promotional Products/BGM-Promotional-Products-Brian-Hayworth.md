### Angle 1（最强）: Premium Metal Category Gap

- **为何成立**: ASI 主流目录偏塑料笔/低端品，premium 金属礼品套装层在小型分销商端普遍空白。Brian 的 hospitality/corporate 客户有"拿得出手的东西"的实际需求，而他当前工具箱里没有这档产品。
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 Executive Set / WGS-008 The Quartet → 酒店VIP客房/企业 year-end gift
- **Hook sentence**: *"Most ASI suppliers stop at plastic pens — if a hotel client asks for an engraved metal pen set that looks like it belongs in a boardroom, I can source that for you at 100-set minimums."*
- **风险**: 不要第一句话报 MOQ 数字。先让产品视觉说话，Brian 见到品质再谈数量。

### Angle 2（稳健）: Hospitality Sector Wedge

- **为何成立**: Brian 本人有酒店管理从业背景，服务 hospitality 客户是其自然客户基础。酒店行业对 amenity gift（executive floor 礼品、客户感谢礼）有持续需求，且对"durable + presentable"要求高于对价格敏感。
- **产品配对**: WGS-006 Onboarding Set（check-in 欢迎礼）/ WP-101 精密圆珠笔（房间留笔）/ WGS-005 Executive（高管楼层）
- **Hook sentence**: *"I notice you've worked in hotel management — we make custom metal amenity gift sets that hotel groups use for executive-floor programs and client appreciation, ready in 30 days from artwork approval."*
- **风险**: Hospitality 客户有紧固的季节性时间节点，务必在开场就说清楚"需提前25–35天规划"，避免 last-minute 单误判。

### Angle 3（长期）: Pilot Reference Partnership

- **为何成立**: 多伦多 ASI 圈子紧密，一次成功首单的转介绍价值远超单笔交易。Brian 有 Photoshop/Illustrator 能力，可自做 mockup 推给客户，降低成交摩擦，也意味着他有动力展示新品类给客户。
- **产品配对**: 100-set pilot of 金属笔 or 桌面套装；提供 client-ready product photos + spec sheet 供其客户 pitch 用。
- **Hook sentence**: *"If you ever need a China-direct metal gift option you can present to clients as something more substantial than the standard ASI catalog, let's run one project together and I'll make it easy on your end."*
- **风险**: 首单体验必须完美，否则转介绍价值归零。不要以"便宜中国选项"定位；强调材质质量和交期可靠性。
