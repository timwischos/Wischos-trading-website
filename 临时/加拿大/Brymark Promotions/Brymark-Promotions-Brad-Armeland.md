### Angle 1（最强）: Premium metal inserts for corporate gift boxes
- **为何成立**: Brymark 官网明确做 corporate gift boxes、sourcing、kitting、recipient shipping。Wischos 金属件可成为礼盒中高感知价值的 durable insert。
- **产品配对**: WGS-005 / WGS-006 / WGS-008；brass pen + metal bookmark + stainless/titanium drinkware + custom box。
- **Hook sentence**: *"I noticed Brymark builds corporate gift boxes and handles sourcing, kitting, and recipient shipping; we support planned custom metal pieces for gift-box programs beyond standard catalogue items."*
- **风险**: 不可像普通目录供应商；必须强调 planned / custom / beyond catalogue。

- **为何成立**: Brymark 公开内容强调 trade-show tiered swag；Wischos 更适合高意向客户/高价值客户的 premium tier，而非 mass giveaway。
- **产品配对**: custom-shaped metal keepsake、titanium EDC、premium pen set、metal desk item、drinkware gift set。
- **Hook sentence**: *"For tiered trade-show programs, we can help with the premium Tier 2/Tier 3 items where perceived value matters more than lowest unit cost."*
- **风险**: MOQ 100 和 25-35 天只适合提前规划活动。

### Angle 3（长期）: 政府/机构认可品专项
