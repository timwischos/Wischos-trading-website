- **产品配对**: brass/stainless/titanium pens、desk pieces、drinkware、small executive gift sets。

- **产品配对**: boxed pen sets、drinkware + desk piece sets、client appreciation / executive gift sets。

### Angle 3（长期）: Brand collab / incentive project metal pieces
