## 三个切入角度（优先级排序）

### Angle 1（最强）: 为premium gift pack加金属层

- **为何成立**: Brandright明确列"premium gift packs"服务项；自有激光雕刻 → 无需新设备即可处理金属件；当前产品线无金属礼品套件，是直接空白
- **产品配对**: WP-101/102（金属笔）入门；WGS-005（Executive Set）中高端；WP-201–208（桌面件）配套
- **Hook sentence**: *"Your laser setup is already there — we can add a metal gift set range that lets you quote NZD $100+ corporate gift orders without adding a new branding process."*
- **风险**: Brandright企业客户可能已有固定礼品供应商；Sistema标签可能限制品类认知

### Angle 2（稳健）: 配合不锈钢水壶线延伸

- **为何成立**: Brandright已卖不锈钢保温杯；买家已接受金属材质；金属桌面件/EDC件是自然套件延伸
- **产品配对**: WP-401–406（金属饮品件）+ WP-301–308（EDC）作为组合推荐
- **Hook sentence**: *"You're already sourcing stainless steel bottles for corporate gifting — we can extend that to matching desk sets and EDC kits, all laser-engravable, same client, higher order value."*
- **风险**: 可能商品化pitch；如Angle 1无反应再用

### Angle 3（长期/季节性）: Q4企业礼品预采购

- **为何成立**: NZ企业8–9月锁定Q4礼品供应商；Wischos 25–35天交期覆盖11月交货；现在（5月）过早，7–8月重触最佳
- **产品配对**: WGS-005/WGS-006作为modular选项，快速报价
- **Hook sentence**: *"If you're building Q4 gift options for corporate clients right now, we can have samples quoted and lead time confirmed within 48 hours — ready for your August proposals."*
- **风险**: 5月过早；此角度留存至Q3 2026再激活
