## 采购背景档案

### 核心业务方向

Gear 是新西兰 owner-led 的 uniforms + promotional merchandise 服务商，核心方向是为企业、学校、社区、NFP、团队和多站点组织提供制服、工作服、品牌商品、促销品、活动物料、线上订购店铺、仓储和全国 fulfilment。它不是纯礼品进口商，而是 programme / fulfilment 型 promo partner。

| 业务方向 | 说明 | 对采购的含义 |
|---|---|---|
| Uniforms / workwear / sports gear | 制服、工装、运动队服、学校团队服装 | 日常以本地/区域服装和装饰供应链为主，不是 Wischos 主攻 |
| Promotional merchandise | pens、drinkware、keyrings/tools、office、events、USB/tech 等 | 存在金属相邻品类，但标准目录占比高 |
| Multi-site programmes | customised ordering platforms、warehousing、fulfilment | 适合引入可重复的高质感 metal gift SKU |
| APAC sourcing | 用户 LinkedIn 明确写 international sourcing across Asia-Pacific、supplier communication/QC/logistics | 说明 Sophie 理解海外供应链，合作沟通门槛较低 |
| Purpose-led / community support | 支持 tamariki、rangatahi、community、kaupapa-led organisations | 可切入 durable / meaningful / recognition gifts，而非 cheap giveaways |

### 潜在采购需求

**高可能需求**
- 100+ units planned custom metal gift sets for corporate campaigns。
- recognition / staff engagement / VIP event metal keepsakes。
- branded portal add-on products: metal pens, desk pieces, drinkware gift sets。
- custom medals / lapel pins / custom-shaped metal pieces for school/team/community events。
- premium client or sponsor gifts that sit beyond commodity catalogue merch。

**中等可能需求**
- APAC custom sourcing backup when NZ/local catalogue cannot meet material, finish or packaging needs。
- multi-site campaign gift inserts paired with uniforms or event merchandise。
- nonprofit/community campaign keepsakes with durable metal presentation。

**低优先级需求**
- low-cost no-MOQ pens。
- daily uniforms, workwear, apparel, school/team clothing。
- urgent local replenishment and stock-sample-driven orders。
- cheap giveaway items where FOB USD18-50/set is unrealistic。
