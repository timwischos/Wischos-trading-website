## Part 2: New Product Recommendations

Organized by strategic priority.

---

### Tier A — High Impact, Fills Documented Search Gaps

**A1. Stainless Steel Slim Card Case**
- What it is: A thin (5mm or less) stainless steel or titanium business card holder — pure card case, no RFID, no badge function. Two-panel spring-loaded or friction-fit design, holds 10–15 cards.
- Why: "Card holder" appears in the top 3 searched gift combinations across all tiers. WP-301 is an RFID wallet + badge holder (bulkier, functional). A slim card case is a different product — thinner, more elegant, better for C-suite recipients who use printed business cards.
- Market demand: "Metal business card holder engraved" is a documented search phrase in buyer intent research.
- FOB estimate: $5–12/unit
- Engraving: Laser engraving on front face; can carry company logo or recipient's initials
- Fits into: WGS-007 as variant, WGS-008 as alternative component, or standalone product

**A2. Stainless Steel Full-Size Travel Flask (350–500ml)**
- What it is: A standard vacuum-insulated travel flask in SUS304 stainless steel, 350–500ml, screw cap or push-button lid. The "daily office bottle" that WP-403 (tumbler) doesn't cover — this is for the recipient who carries it in a bag.
- Why: Drinkware is ASI's #2 category. WP-403 is desk-optimized (wide base, heavy). WP-401 is pure titanium (premium price). There's no standard stainless travel flask at a mid-price point ($10–20 FOB). This fills the most searched drinkware slot.
- Market demand: Tumbler + pen + card holder searches universally assume a travel flask format.
- FOB estimate: $10–18/unit
- Engraving options: Laser engraving on body, or full-color dye-sublimation
- Fits into: WGS-007 as a travel variant, or new set with pen + flask + key organizer

**A3. Metal USB Flash Drive (16–64GB)**
- What it is: A solid aluminum or stainless steel body USB flash drive, 16–64GB, USB-A + USB-C dual port. Laser engraved body. Cap or sliding mechanism.
- Why: "Pen + card holder + USB flash drive" is a documented Tier 1 search combo ("tech-functional" trio). No USB product exists in Wischos's lineup. USB drives remain a corporate staple despite cloud storage — especially for conference handouts and trade show contexts where files are shared on-site.
- Market demand: The tech-functional gift set is one of the three most searched corporate gift combos globally.
- FOB estimate: $6–15/unit (depending on GB)
- Fits into: New tech-focused gift set (pen + USB + card holder), or add to WGS-006 as an option
- Note: Verify supplier reliability — USB internals vary widely in quality; insist on Sandisk or Samsung flash chip

**A4. Metal Multi-Tool (Pocket Size)**
- What it is: A compact metal multi-tool in the 6–12 function range — screwdrivers, bottle opener, ruler, wire stripper, box cutter, hex wrench. Titanium or stainless body. ~90mm folded length.
- Why: EDC items (multi-tools, pocket knives) are nearly absent from Western B2B gifting pages — identified as a white-space differentiation angle in the competitor research. Wischos already has pry bar (WP-305) and folding scissors (WP-307), but no true multi-tool. A multi-tool broadens the EDC category appeal to a much wider professional audience (not just field workers).
- Market demand: Under-represented in competitor catalogs = less competition, differentiation opportunity.
- FOB estimate: $12–22/unit
- Fits into: New EDC set (multi-tool + tactical pen + key organizer), or as standalone

### Tier B — Medium Impact, Expands Range

**B1. Aluminium Luggage / Bag Tag**
- What it is: A solid aluminium rectangle tag (75×40mm approx.) with a stainless steel cable loop, laser-engraved on front and back. A clean, modern alternative to plastic and leather luggage tags.
- Why: Travel corporate gifts appear at #7 in use-case rankings (conference delegate gifts, company anniversary gifts). No travel-adjacent product exists in the lineup. Very easy to engrave with company logo or recipient name — high personalization appeal.
- FOB estimate: $4–8/unit
- Fits into: A conference delegate gift set (pen + luggage tag + card case)
- Bonus: Appears in airport environments, giving brand exposure in non-office contexts

**B2. Stainless Steel Desk Coaster / Coaster Set**
- What it is: A set of 4 stainless steel or brass coasters, 90mm diameter, with a stacking holder. Laser-engraved logo on top face.
- Why: Desk coasters appear in competitor executive gift catalogs (Crestline, 4imprint executive tier). They sit on desks permanently, providing long-term brand exposure. Current lineup has no coaster.
- FOB estimate: $15–25 for a 4-piece set with holder
- Fits into: New "Desk Collection" set (coasters + pen holder + letter opener)
- Note: Best for C-suite gifting where recipients have their own office; less relevant for open-plan teams

**B3. Titanium / Stainless Pill/Supplement Case**
- What it is: A cylindrical or capsule-shaped titanium or stainless steel pill/supplement organizer, 60–80mm length, screw-cap. Laser-engraved.
- Why: Wellness in corporate gifting is growing. Healthcare is ASI's #2 industry buyer. A titanium pill case bridges the wellness trend with the material integrity story — and it's genuinely useful daily. The EDC wellness angle is underexplored.
- FOB estimate: $8–14/unit
- Fits into: Wellness/health-sector gift set, or EDC lifestyle set

**B4. Metal Keychain Fob (Branded)**
- What it is: A solid brass or stainless steel keychain fob — a simple disc or bar shape, heavy, laser-engraved on both faces. Not a functional tool, purely a premium branded keychain.
- Why: Keychain appears in virtually every Tier 3 onboarding combo (tumbler + notebook + pen + keychain). WP-302 is a key organizer (functional). WP-308 is a titanium keychain (functional + bottle opener). A simple, elegant branded keychain fob — heavy, satisfying, minimal — fills a different slot. Recipients keep it for years. Brand stays on their keys permanently.
- FOB estimate: $3–7/unit
- Fits into: WGS-006B as 4th component option, any onboarding kit

### Tier C — Niche, Consider After Growth

**C1. Aluminium Wireless Charging Pad**
- What it is: An aluminium-body 15W Qi wireless charging pad for desk use. USB-C input, LED ring, laser-engraved logo zone.
- Why: "Pen + tumbler + wireless charger" is a Tier 2 tech-forward combo. A metal-body wireless charger fits the all-metal brand story. WP-208 device stand could pair with it.
- FOB estimate: $14–22/unit
- Risk: Electronics quality control is harder than pure metal goods. Qi certification required.

**C2. Metal Pen Tray / Desk Organizer**
- What it is: A flat aluminium or brass tray, ~200mm × 80mm, with 1–3 compartment dividers. Catches pens, cards, keys, coins. Laser-engraved.
- Why: WP-202 is a pen holder (vertical). A horizontal tray is a different form — lower profile, more display. Common in high-end desk setups.
- FOB estimate: $12–20/unit
- Fits into: "Desk Collection" set alongside coasters and pen holder

**C3. Metal Water Bottle (Sport Cap, 600–750ml)**
- What it is: A single-wall stainless steel sport bottle with metal push-button sport cap, 600–750ml. For active/outdoor corporate gifting.
- Why: Health and fitness corporate gifting is growing. WP-404 is a campfire mug (niche). This fills the standard "athlete" or "active lifestyle" corporate gift slot.
- FOB estimate: $8–14/unit
- Risk: Competitive category — Stanley, Yeti dominate mindshare in Canada/UK. Harder to position against brand-name alternatives.
