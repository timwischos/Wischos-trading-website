> The inkless tip pen writes on most surfaces without ink cartridges, lasts 20,000m, and tends to stop recipients who have never encountered one. ### Expert Note 2

**EN Title：** Why a letter opener in 2026

### Expert Note 3

**EN Title：** Why Kraft Rigid Box?

**EN Body：**
> A kraft rigid box uses 1200gsm+ greyboard wrapped in unbleached kraft paper. It delivers solid unboxing weight — the lid lifts with deliberate resistance — at 30–40% less cost than laminated art-paper boxes. The natural material finish works well alongside the inkless pen: both are functional without being decorative.

**中文直译：**
牛皮纸硬盒采用1200克以上灰板外包未漂白牛皮纸。开箱有实质重量感——盖子以刻意的阻力抬起——成本比覆膜铜版纸盒低30-40%。天然材质表面与无墨笔搭配良好：两者都是功能性而非装饰性。

---

### Expert Note 4

**EN Title：** Logo on Packaging: Hot Foil Stamping

**EN Body：**
> For kraft surfaces, hot foil stamping (gold, silver, or copper) creates the highest contrast and perceived value. The foil fuses into the paper fibers under heat and pressure, producing a metallic imprint that is scratch-resistant and does not peel. Alternative: debossing (blind stamp without foil) creates an elegant tone-on-tone tactile logo — quieter but highly refined.

**中文直译：**
牛皮纸表面，烫金（金、银或铜色）创造最高对比度和感知价值。箔膜在热压下融入纸纤维，产生耐刮不脱落的金属印记。替代方案：压凹（无箔盲压）创造优雅的同色调触感logo——更低调但高度精致。

---

### Expert Note 5

**EN Title：** Upgrade Option: Magnetic Closure Box

**EN Body：**
> For clients seeking a higher-tier presentation, upgrading to a magnetic closure box (art paper + embedded magnets) adds approximately $1.50–2.50/unit at MOQ 100. The magnetic snap creates an audible "click" on opening — a small detail that significantly elevates the unboxing ritual and gift perceived value.

**中文直译：**
对于寻求更高档次展示的客户，升级为磁扣盒（铜版纸+内嵌磁铁）在MOQ 100时每件大约增加$1.50-2.50。磁扣开合产生清晰的"咔哒"声——一个小细节，显著提升开箱仪式感和礼品感知价值。

**EN Body：**

EVA density is typically 38–45 kg/m³ for gift packaging: firm enough to hold components in place, soft enough to absorb transit shock.

**中文直译：**
