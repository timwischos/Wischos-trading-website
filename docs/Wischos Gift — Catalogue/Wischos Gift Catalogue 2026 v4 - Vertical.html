Wischos GiftLogo Customisation

Every surface.
Precisely marked.

01 / Laser Marking
Laser engraving · annealing · etching
Permanent monochrome mark on brass, stainless steel, titanium, and anodised aluminium. Solid colour fills and fine line work both possible. Used on pen barrels, key organisers, money clips, and tumbler bodies.
02 / Colour Marking
UV printing · silk-screen · pad print
Full-colour or spot-colour brand marks on flat metal surfaces and packaging. Used when the brand requires Pantone match or multi-colour artwork on the product or gift box.
03 / Packaging Branding
Hot foil · blind deboss · printed sleeve
Hot foil stamping in gold/silver/copper, blind deboss for understated elegance, full-colour printed sleeves and inserts. Often combined with rigid box, magnetic closure, or tin can packaging.
04 / Upgrade Options
Soft-touch · printed liner · wood sleeve · waxed canvas pouch
Premium finish options that elevate the unboxing experience — soft-touch lamination on box exteriors, printed inside lid, wood-grain veneer sleeves, waxed canvas drawstring pouches for EDC sets.
