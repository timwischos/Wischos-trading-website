# Corporate Gifting Platforms — Competitive Intelligence
*Research date: 2026-04-25 | Source: Agent research on Sendoso, Snappy, Goody, Alyce, Reachdesk*

## Summary: Competitor or Distribution Channel?

**These platforms do NOT compete with Wischos Gift — they are potential distribution channels.**

They are software companies ($10,000–$60,000/year platform fees). They need products in their catalogs. They are not manufacturers or metal gift specialists. They actively seek suppliers.

**Structural mismatch to solve first:** These platforms are built for per-unit sends. Wischos's current model (MOQ 100, custom engraving, 25–35 day lead time) is incompatible. A pre-made stock line would be required.

---

## Platform 1 — Sendoso

**Model:** SaaS "Sending Management Platform" — curates third-party vendors (30,000+ items). Subscription: $22,250–$60,450/year enterprise.

**Product categories:** Food, wine, candles, journals, leather goods, tech accessories. Metal gift sets are rare.

**Price range:** eGifts $25–$50; executive physical gifts implied $75–$200+.

**Target buyer:** B2B sales/marketing/SDR teams at mid-to-enterprise SaaS. Integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft.

**Key use cases:** Pipeline acceleration, ABM, customer retention, employee appreciation, onboarding.

**International:** Fulfillment in US, Canada, UK, EU, APAC. eGifts to ~50 countries.

**Vendor program:** Closed / invitation-based. Contact via form; selective curation. Not easily accessible without referral or US retail presence.

**Priority for Wischos: Low** — US-dominated, closed vendor access.

---

## Platform 2 — Snappy

**Model:** Recipient-choice gifting — sender picks budget, recipient chooses from curated menu. Brand Partner program: brands fulfill their own orders per Snappy's SLA. Acquired Covver (swag channel) in 2025.

**Product categories:** Lifestyle consumer brands — YETI, North Face, Moleskine, Hydro Flask. "Little Luxuries" (Gucci, Baccarat, Tom Ford). No custom metal B2B sets.

**Price range:** $25–$5,000+; most corporate programs $50–$200 per recipient.

**Target buyer:** HR teams (employee milestones, onboarding), marketing, sales. Integrates with Workday, BambooHR, CRMs.

**International:** 176 countries, 30 countries with local sourcing. Canada and UK explicitly supported.

**Vendor program:** Open public application at snappy.com/business/partner-program-apply. Vendors fulfill own orders. Dispatch SLA: 1–3 business days. Per-unit fulfillment model (no bulk).

**Priority for Wischos: Medium** — accessible entry point, but 1–3 day dispatch SLA is incompatible with made-to-order model. A stock line would be needed.

---

## Platform 3 — Goody

**Model:** "Gift as a text" — recipient picks from curated catalog. Freemium (free tier US/Canada; Pro ~$20/user/month for international). 400+ premium brands. Raised $32M (NEA, SoftBank, Index).

**Product categories:** Food, wellness, home goods, tech (AirPods, Ember mugs, Therabody). Consumer-brand-oriented. No metal corporate gift sets.

**Price range:** $15–$1,000+; common tiers $25, $50, $100.

**Target buyer:** Sales, HR, marketing — SMB-friendly. 20,000+ companies use it.

**International:** Canada (Starter plan); 140+ countries (Pro plan). Ships from US warehouses internationally — no local UK/UAE infrastructure.

**Vendor program:** No public application. Appears invitation-based. Low priority.

**Priority for Wischos: Low** — primarily US consumer-oriented, closed vendor path.

---

## Platform 4 — Alyce (now Alyce by Sendoso)

**Model:** Acquired by Sendoso in February 2024. "Power of Choice" — recipients can accept, exchange, or donate. 30,000-item marketplace. AI-enhanced personalization.

**Target buyer:** B2B marketing, sales, customer success at mid-to-enterprise SaaS/tech. Very similar ICP to Sendoso.

**International:** Gift cards in 100+ countries; physical gifts depend on supplier network.

**Vendor program:** Closed, invitation-based. Now managed through Sendoso's combined infrastructure.

**Priority for Wischos: Low** — merged into Sendoso, same access challenges.

---

## Platform 5 — Reachdesk ⭐ Best Fit

**Model:** Global B2B gifting + swag platform with own warehouse network (US, Canada, UK, EU, APAC). Combines marketplace with actual warehousing. Ships to 180+ countries. UK-headquartered. Founded 2018.

**Product categories:** 8,500+ options from local vendors worldwide — snacks, cocktail kits, wellness, custom swag, gift baskets. Metal gifts appear in engraved bottles and branded items but not as a primary category.

**Price range:** Not publicly listed per-item. Bulk swag sourcing minimum: $2,500. Platform pricing on request.

**Target buyer:** B2B marketing, sales, customer success at mid-to-enterprise. UK and EU are primary markets.

**Key use cases:** Pipeline acceleration, ABM, event gifting, employee recognition, customer retention, direct mail.

**International:** ✅ Warehouses in UK, Canada, US, EU, APAC. Best-in-class UK/Canada coverage among all platforms.

**Vendor program:** **Most open of all platforms.** Public page: reachdesk.com/become-a-vendor. Contact: marketplace@reachdesk.com. Benefits: no long-term contracts, no hidden fees, straightforward revenue share, platform handles payment and brand exposure, dedicated vendor onboarding. No minimum product requirements or complex technical setup.

**Priority for Wischos: HIGH** — UK-headquartered, open vendor application, exact target markets (UK, Canada, UAE), accessible contact.

---

## What a Reachdesk Partnership Would Look Like

**Requirement:** Maintain a small stock of hero sets (e.g., WGS-005 Executive Set) in an accessible location — either shipped to a Reachdesk UK warehouse in advance, or fulfilled from China with a declared 10–14 day lead time (negotiable for premium custom items).

**Revenue model:** Reachdesk lists the product at a marked-up retail price. Orders come per-unit (breaks current MOQ model). Lower per-unit revenue, but compounding volume and UK brand exposure.

**Structural blocker:** Custom MOQ 100 + 25–35 day lead time + made-to-order engraving is incompatible with per-unit fulfillment expectations. A pre-manufactured, non-engraved stock set that buyers can optionally customize would solve this.

---

## Near-Term Recommendation

**Don't pursue platform partnerships now.** Use this research as competitive intelligence and positioning reference. The supply chain structure (custom MOQ, long lead time) is the blocker.

**Trigger to revisit:** Once Wischos has a steady factory relationship and can hold buffer stock of 2–3 hero SKUs → contact Reachdesk at marketplace@reachdesk.com with a sample product and the UK-local-sourcing angle.
