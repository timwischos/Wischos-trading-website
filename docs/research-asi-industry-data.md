## 3. Premium / Executive Gift Segment

No dedicated revenue figure from ASI/PPAI, but:

- **Industry shift toward premiumization** — distributors advising clients to "buy fewer, higher-quality items"; documented trend driven by sustainability pressure + client fatigue with cheap promo
- **Executive gift spend sweet spot:** $25–$125 per item (Giftsenda 2025); 77% of B2B gift buyers spend in this range for one-to-one gifting
- **Premium use cases:** Company stores, executive onboarding kits, client retention, award programs
- **Global corporate gifting market** (broader, includes luxury): $839.6B in 2024, growing at 9.6% CAGR

**Wischos positioning:** $18–$50 FOB → ~$40–$115 distributor sell price (2.2–2.5x markup). This sits squarely in the $25–$125 documented sweet spot.

## 4. Price Per Unit Benchmarks

- **B2B executive gift spend:** $50–$125 per recipient (documented premium tier)
- **General corporate gift range:** $20–$100 per recipient
- **Drinkware (distributor cost):** $8–$25; retail $18–$55
- **Pens:** Entry-level metal pen ~$1 cost; premium branded pens $5–$20+ distributor cost
- **Distributor margin:** 35–40% gross margin typical → a $50 end-buyer price = ~$30–$32 distributor cost

## 6. Seasonal Patterns

| Quarter | Activity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Q4 (Oct–Dec) | **Peak** | Holiday/year-end gifts; use-it-or-lose-it budgets |
| Q3 (Jul–Sep) | Secondary peak | Trade show season; Q4 order placement window |
| Q2 (Apr–Jun) | Moderate | Employee recognition, graduation |
| Q1 (Jan–Mar) | Slowest | Post-holiday freeze |

**Critical implication:** Buyers plan Q4 delivery 90–120 days out → **pitch Canada/UK buyers in July–August** for December delivery. Wischos's 25–35 day production fits within a 90-day Q4 planning cycle if orders land by end of September.

## 7. Distributor vs. Direct Sourcing Trends

- **61%+ of US distributors sourced direct from overseas manufacturers in 2024**, up from 47% in 2022
- **88% of promo products are imported from China**
- **2025 tariff disruption:** Production diversification to Mexico, India, Thailand accelerating — but Canada and UK face lower tariff exposure on Chinese goods than the US
- **Trend: fewer, better suppliers** — distributors consolidating their roster toward suppliers with category depth and reliable quality
- **"Sourcing Beyond China"** is a 2025 ASI Counselor SOI chapter topic

**Wischos implication:** Direct-sourcing trend = your buyer profile. Canada/UK/UAE targeting is strategically sound precisely because tariff exposure is lower than US. Single-contact model + curated catalog aligns with the "fewer suppliers" consolidation trend.
