# Image Prompts and Placement

Article: `Discreet Branding Corporate Gifts Why Smaller Logos Often Work Better - MIXED STYLE.md`

## Recommended Image Plan

Use one hero image plus three supporting article images. If the page needs to stay lighter, keep only the hero image, the big-logo-vs-small-logo comparison image and the metal detail image.

## 1. Hero Image

Place directly below the article title and before the opening paragraph.

**Purpose:** Introduce the article's core idea: premium corporate gifts can carry a brand without looking like advertisements.

**Prompt:**

```text
A premium corporate gift arrangement on a quiet executive desk: a brushed stainless steel card holder, a solid brass pen, and a matte aluminum phone stand, each with very subtle tone-on-tone laser engraved branding that is barely visible unless the light catches it. Soft natural window light, close-up product photography, shallow depth of field, restrained composition, neutral grey and warm brass tones, no large logos, no text, no hands, high-end B2B gifting mood, realistic materials, editorial product photography.
```

**中文提示词：**

```text
安静的高管办公桌上的高端企业礼品组合：拉丝不锈钢名片夹、实心黄铜笔、哑光铝合金手机支架，每件产品都有非常克制的同色系激光雕刻品牌标识，只有在光线掠过表面时才隐约可见。柔和自然窗光，近距离产品摄影，浅景深，克制构图，中性灰与温暖黄铜色调，没有大 logo，没有文字，没有手，高端 B2B 礼品氛围，真实材质，编辑风格产品摄影。
```

## 2. Big Logo vs Small Logo Comparison

Place after the section `Why Loud Branding Gets in the Way`.

**Purpose:** Make the article's main argument visually obvious: a product can become harder to use when the logo dominates it.

**Prompt:**

```text
Split-scene product photography comparing two corporate gift approaches: on the left, a cheap promotional item with an oversized bright logo, slightly unattractive and unused; on the right, a premium brushed metal desk accessory with a small tone-on-tone engraved logo, placed naturally on a working desk. Realistic lighting, professional B2B editorial style, no readable text, no brand names, subtle contrast, not cartoonish.
```

**中文提示词：**

```text
左右分屏的产品摄影，对比两种企业礼品方式：左侧是一个廉价促销品，带有过大的高亮 logo，略显廉价且像被闲置；右侧是一个高端拉丝金属桌面配件，带有小面积同色系雕刻 logo，自然放置在真实工作桌面上。真实光线，专业 B2B 编辑风格，没有可读文字，没有品牌名，克制对比，不要卡通感。
```

## 3. Packaging-Led Branding

Place after the subsection `Let the Packaging Do More Work` inside `Better Ways to Brand the Gift`.

**Purpose:** Show that the packaging can carry the brand while the product itself stays clean and usable.

**Prompt:**

```text
Premium corporate gift unboxing scene: a clean rigid gift box, insert card, sleeve, and a metal pen or card holder inside. The packaging has subtle branding, while the product itself is almost logo-free. Elegant B2B client gift presentation, soft shadows, natural light, restrained colors, no readable text, no exaggerated luxury props, realistic product photography.
```

**中文提示词：**

```text
高端企业礼品开箱场景：干净的硬质礼品盒、插卡、封套，盒内有金属笔或金属名片夹。包装上有克制的品牌细节，而产品本身几乎没有可见 logo。优雅的 B2B 客户礼品呈现，柔和阴影，自然光，克制配色，没有可读文字，没有夸张奢华道具，真实产品摄影。
```

## Method-Specific Product Image Prompts

Use these when the article needs smaller supporting images for each logo-reduction method. These images should feel like product documentation from a premium sourcing partner, not decorative stock photography.

### Method 1: Move the Logo Away From the Main Surface

**Recommended placement:** Inside `Better Ways to Brand the Gift`, after `Move the Logo Away From the Main Surface`.

**Purpose:** Show that a product can still be branded without placing the logo on the most visible face.

**Prompt:**

```text
Close-up product photograph of a premium brushed metal card holder on a clean office desk. The main front surface is blank and refined, while a tiny laser-engraved logo is placed on the lower back corner or side edge, only visible from a slight angle. Emphasis on clean surface, edge detail, restrained branding, professional B2B gifting aesthetic, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, no readable text, no large logo, realistic photography.
```

**中文提示词：**

```text
干净办公桌上一只高端拉丝金属名片夹的近距离产品照片。正面主视觉面保持干净留白，极小的激光雕刻 logo 放在背面下角或侧边边缘，只有从轻微角度才能看到。重点表现干净表面、边缘细节、克制品牌标识、专业 B2B 礼品审美，柔和自然光，浅景深，没有可读文字，没有大 logo，真实摄影。
```
