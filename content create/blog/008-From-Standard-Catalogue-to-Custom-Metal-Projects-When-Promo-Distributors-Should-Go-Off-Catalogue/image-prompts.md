# Image Plan and Prompts

Article: `From Standard Catalogue to Custom Metal Projects: When Promo Distributors Should Go Off-Catalogue`

Visual direction: practical B2B distributor workflow, not luxury lifestyle. Images should show product decision-making, catalogue vs custom contrast, metal material details, and project brief planning. Avoid generic office stock photos, fake dashboards, excessive gradients, and over-stylized luxury scenes.

## Image 1 - Hero Image

**Placement:** Top hero / blog cover  
**Purpose:** Show the central contrast: standard catalogue products on one side, custom metal gift project development on the other.  
**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9  
**Filename suggestion:** `hero-catalogue-to-custom-metal-projects.avif`
**Generated file:** `hero-catalogue-to-custom-metal-projects.png`

**Prompt:**

```text
Photorealistic editorial B2B product photography for a promotional products distributor. A clean worktable split into two visual zones: on the left, a printed promotional products catalogue with generic logo items such as a mug, tote bag and basic pen; on the right, premium custom metal gift project components including a brass pen, stainless desk accessory, metal keychain, lapel pin, small medal, packaging insert card and rigid gift box. Neutral modern office lighting, realistic materials, professional sourcing desk, no visible brand logos, no people, no text overlays, sharp focus, premium but practical, 16:9.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No fake brand names, no readable logos, no luxury jewelry styling, no cartoon look, no cluttered desk, no exaggerated gold shine, no text labels.
```

## Optional Diagram - Decision Flow

Use this as a manually designed HTML/CSS or vector diagram rather than an AI image if the final page layout supports it, so text stays crisp.

```text
Client brief
  -> Rush / low-cost / broad handout?
      -> Standard catalogue route
  -> Planned / higher perceived value / specific material or packaging?
      -> Custom metal project review
  -> Fit check: MOQ, timeline, budget, proofing, destination
      -> Quote / mockup / feasibility feedback
```

Design notes:

- Use simple rectangular decision nodes.
- Keep text short.
- Neutral grey background, Wischos red accent only for the custom route.
- Avoid decorative icons unless they improve scanning.
