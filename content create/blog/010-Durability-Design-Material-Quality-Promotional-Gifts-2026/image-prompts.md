# Image Prompts — Durability, Design and Material Quality in Promotional Gifts 2026

## Hero Image

High-end editorial product photography for a B2B corporate gifting blog. Arrange four custom metal promotional gifts on a clean warm-gray surface: a brushed stainless steel bottle opener, a solid brass pen, a matte aluminum folding phone stand, and a titanium keychain or compact bottle. Lighting should reveal metal texture, weight, machined edges, and restrained laser engraving. No hands, no people, no visible brand logos other than subtle generic engraving. Premium but practical, not luxury jewelry. Aspect ratio 16:9, suitable for blog hero.

## Material Comparison Image

Top-down comparison image of four metal material samples and related small gift objects: brass, stainless steel, aluminum, and titanium. Each piece should have a distinct finish: brushed brass, brushed stainless, anodized matte aluminum, stone-washed or satin titanium. Include a simple neutral background, precise shadows, and enough spacing for possible text labels added later. The image should support a section explaining durability, design, material quality, and hand feel. Aspect ratio 4:3.

## Branding Detail Image

Macro product photo showing restrained logo application on metal: tone-on-tone laser engraving on brushed stainless steel, small clip engraving on a brass pen, and subtle engraving on matte aluminum. Focus on engraving clarity and surface texture. The mood should be technical and premium, with shallow depth of field and realistic metal reflections. No oversized logos, no decorative clutter. Aspect ratio 3:2.
