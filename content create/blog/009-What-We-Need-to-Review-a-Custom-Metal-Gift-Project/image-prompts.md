# Image Plan and Prompts

Article: `What We Need to Review a Custom Metal Gift Project`

Visual direction: practical B2B sourcing review, not luxury gift advertising. Images should show how a rough custom metal gift idea becomes a reviewable project: reference product, quantity/budget/deadline notes, metal samples, logo method, packaging and destination planning. Keep the mood professional, calm and useful. Avoid fake brand names, readable documents, exaggerated luxury styling, decorative office stock photos, and text-heavy AI graphics.

## Image 1 - Hero Image

**Placement:** Top hero / blog cover, before `You Do Not Need a Complete Specification to Start`  
**Purpose:** Show the central promise of the article: an incomplete custom metal gift idea can still be reviewed when the key project signals are present.  
**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9  
**Filename suggestion:** `hero-custom-metal-gift-project-review.avif`

**Prompt:**

```text
Photorealistic editorial B2B sourcing desk for a custom metal gift project review. On a clean neutral worktable: a brass pen, stainless steel card holder, small metal keychain, titanium drinkware sample, material swatches, a simple blank project note sheet, calendar, calculator, packaging sample box, and a laptop with an unreadable product reference page. The scene should feel like an early supplier review of an incomplete but workable client idea. Practical, organized, premium but not luxury, natural office light, realistic metal textures, no people, no readable text, no brand logos, no fake company names, 16:9.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No stock photo handshake, no luxury jewelry styling, no readable forms, no fake logos, no messy paperwork, no dark dramatic lighting, no text overlays.
```

## Image 2 - Rough Idea to Reviewable Project

**Placement:** Near `You Do Not Need a Complete Specification to Start`, after the first short-direction examples  
**Purpose:** Reinforce that the customer does not need a full specification; rough product direction plus quantity and date can begin the conversation.  
**Recommended aspect ratio:** 4:3  
**Filename suggestion:** `rough-idea-to-reviewable-project.avif`

**Prompt:**

```text
Overhead photorealistic image of an early custom gift inquiry being organized. A blank note card with no readable text sits beside a titanium pen set sample, a small calendar with unreadable dates, a quantity counter card with no visible numbers, a shipping carton corner, and metal finish samples in brass, stainless steel and aluminium. The image should suggest a rough idea becoming a reviewable project, without showing actual written information. Clean B2B workspace, soft shadows, practical sourcing mood, no hands, no people, no readable text, no brand marks, 4:3.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No visible email text, no readable handwriting, no checklist with words, no fake UI, no people pointing at papers, no decorative luxury props.
```

## Image 3 - Material, Finish and Logo Review

**Placement:** Near `Logo and Branding Needs`, or between `Product Idea or Reference` and `Logo and Branding Needs` if the page needs an earlier technical visual  
**Purpose:** Show Wischos reviewing material, surface finish and logo method together rather than as separate decisions.  
**Recommended aspect ratio:** 3:2  
**Filename suggestion:** `material-finish-logo-review.avif`

**Prompt:**

```text
Macro editorial product photography showing a custom metal gift branding review. Arrange brushed stainless steel, matte aluminium, brass and titanium samples with subtle generic laser engraving marks, a small logo placement mockup card with no readable logo, color swatches, and a metal pen clip detail. Lighting should reveal engraving depth, brushed texture, anodised finish and realistic metal reflections. Technical but polished B2B sourcing style, shallow depth of field, no real brands, no readable text, no oversized logos, 3:2.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No large fake logo, no Pantone numbers, no readable labels, no jewelry look, no excessive gold shine, no unrealistic laser effects.
```

**Recommended aspect ratio:** 4:3  
**Filename suggestion:** `custom-metal-gift-packaging-review.avif`

**Prompt:**

```text
Photorealistic B2B product packaging review scene for custom metal gifts. Show the same metal pen and card holder displayed in two presentation routes: simple individual packaging on one side, and a rigid gift box with fitted insert, sleeve and blank message card on the other side. Include a neutral shipping carton in the background to imply freight volume. Clean tabletop, practical premium presentation, no visible logos, no readable text, realistic paperboard and metal textures, organized layout, 4:3.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No retail luxury cosmetics styling, no ribbons, no readable package text, no fake brand names, no overdecorated props, no holiday gift clichés.
```

## Image 5 - Destination and Feasibility Review

**Placement:** Near `Destination Country` or `When a Feasibility Review Comes Before a Firm Quote`  
**Purpose:** Connect destination, documentation, shipping method and feasibility review without making a legal/compliance image.  
**Recommended aspect ratio:** 16:9  
**Filename suggestion:** `destination-feasibility-review.avif`

**Prompt:**

```text
Photorealistic export project review desk for custom metal gifts leaving China. A small arranged gift set with brass pen, stainless steel card case and metal keychain sits beside a neutral shipping carton, blank document folder, material sample chips, a simple world map outline with no readable country names, and a calculator. The image should suggest destination, Incoterm, documentation and feasibility review before final quote. Professional sourcing environment, clean neutral colors, realistic lighting, no people, no readable documents, no flags, no logos, 16:9.
```

**Avoid:**

```text
No customs stamps with readable words, no flags, no legal document closeups, no airport cargo scene, no warehouse chaos, no fake shipping labels.
```

## Optional Diagram - Review Inputs

Use this as a manually designed HTML/CSS or vector diagram rather than an AI image if the final page layout supports it, so text stays crisp and editable.

**Placement:** Near `Send What You Already Know`

```text
Product idea or reference
Quantity range
Budget range
Deadline or event date
Logo / branding needs
Packaging expectation
Destination country
        ↓
Feasibility review
        ↓
Quote path / sample route / packaging suggestion
```

Design notes:

- Use a compact vertical flow or two-column input grid.
- Keep labels short and readable.
- Neutral background with one Wischos red accent for the review step.
- Avoid decorative icons unless they improve scanning.
- Do not make it look like a form the customer must complete.
