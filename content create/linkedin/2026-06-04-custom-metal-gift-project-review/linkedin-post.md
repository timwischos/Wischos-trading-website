**Source article:** What We Need to Review a Custom Metal Gift Project  
**Target audience:** Distributors, gifting agencies  
**Tone:** Practical, supplier-to-distributor, direct

---

"My client needs premium metal gifts. Around 200 sets. Q4 delivery."

That's enough to open a sourcing conversation — not a quote, but a direction.

We don't need final artwork or a full spec.

What stalls a review is missing basics — no quantity range, no budget signal, no event date, no sense of what the client wants.

These details move things forward:

→ What the client has in mind — product direction, reference, or use case
→ Quantity range
→ Budget range
→ Client's event date or in-hands deadline
→ Logo and branding requirements
→ Packaging expectation (standard or presentation quality)

We review material, finish, logo method and packaging together — one consolidated direction, not four separate back-and-forths.

Share what you're thinking — we'll explore it together and get it done.
