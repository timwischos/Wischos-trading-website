## English Post

For promo distributors, not every promotional product brief needs off-catalogue custom sourcing.

Standard catalogues are still the right route when the client needs:

-> speed  
-> proven SKUs  
-> low unit cost  
-> repeatable everyday items

But some briefs are different.

For promo distributors, off-catalogue sourcing means moving beyond a standard SKU when the brief needs a specific material, finish, shape, packaging, or gift-set structure.

When a client says:

"We want something people will keep."  
"This is for top customers."  
"It needs to feel more premium."  
"We do not want the usual giveaway item."

That is usually not a commodity order signal.

It is a project signal.

Custom metal gifts can work well in this situation because metal brings:

* weight
* permanence
* perceived value

Think metal pens, desk pieces, pins, medals, EDC items, drinkware, custom-shaped pieces, or planned gift sets.

The key is not choosing catalogue or custom.

The key is knowing when each one fits.

Catalogue = speed and repeatability  
Off-catalogue custom sourcing = planned, higher-perceived-value projects

For Q4 and year-end gifting, timing matters too.

If buyers are planning delivery 90-120 days ahead, there is usually enough room to develop something more specific than another standard giveaway.
